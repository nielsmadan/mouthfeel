# Finnish-influenced English notes

These patterns are authoring prompts assembled from the maintainer's experience working with fluent Finnish developers and the initial calibration discussion. They include discourse habits, word-order transfer, and calques such as `ottaa käyttöön` → “take into use.” They are not quotations or universal claims about Finnish speakers. Use them intermittently and validate them against the evaluation corpus.

Background reading:

- Tarja Nikula, research on Finnish speakers' English: https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED396554.pdf
- Anne Kankaanranta, Finnish workplace English: https://journal.fi/afinlavk/article/view/59864/20876
