# Russian-influenced English notes

These patterns are authoring prompts assembled from the maintainer's experience working with fluent Russian developers. They are not quotations or universal claims about Russian speakers. Keep transfer patterns sparse and validate them with native or highly experienced reviewers before release.

Background reading:

- Linguistic features described in a Russian English study: https://pubs.sciepub.com/education/2/9/20/index.html
- Cambridge overview of Russian English: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/russian-english/linguistic-features-of-russian-english/B4D1AE3FA48E942668EEDC29584A724F
